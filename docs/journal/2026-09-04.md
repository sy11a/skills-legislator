## BL-082 — stage 6, build: T-12 (publish per RID, the startup budget, install, `version --json`, arm integrity)

The task that turns a binary into a release. Its centre was a fact the plan could
not have known without asking the toolchain: `error : Cross-OS native compilation
is not supported`. C-12 describes `publish-legislator.sh` as a loop over the four
RIDs, and that loop cannot run anywhere — three of the four need their own
operating system. The operator ruled option (a): the script publishes the host's
RID and refuses the rest by name, cheaply, before the SDK is reached; the matrix
in `.github/workflows/dotnet.yml` builds all four; and the workflow gets a driver
in `check_static.py` rather than shipping as unverified configuration, since it
is the only builder three of the RIDs have.

Fifteen reds, every one predicted. The edition pin failed with `skill/VERSION='25'
against Version.props major='0'` before either number moved; the build carried
