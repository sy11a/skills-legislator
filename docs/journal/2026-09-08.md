## BL-082 — F-1 closed on option (a): the case converges

- The operator ruled (a): restore the guard and make silence the contract.
  `hooks.json` now runs `command -v legislator >/dev/null 2>&1 || exit 0; exec
  legislator hook <name>`. Both requirement texts moved with the behaviour —
  R-8215's "one warning" to "silently", R-8208's "never an interpreter" to "the
  hook lives in the binary and in no interpreter, behind a shell guard" — with
  the clarification in the spec and the reasoning in ADR-0011. Amending a
  requirement is not softening it here: the sentence said "one warning" and the
  delivered thing warned on every keystroke, so either the text or the code was
  going to move, and the text was the half that was wrong about its own intent.
- Red first, and the red was the finding itself: `exit=127 stderr='sh: line 1:
  legislator: command not found'` against the unchanged file, plus four shape
  assertions. The twin drives the real command line as a process through a PATH
  holding a shell and no arm — the property belongs to the command line, not to
  any hook the suite can call in-process. Its broken pipe on stdin is caught and
  read as success: the guard gave up before reading, which is the whole point.

- **✅ Converged.** Three findings raised by the converge, three closed. What is
