## What the two scenarios proved about the edition

- The new check fires end-to-end through a fresh agent following SKILL.md:
  the report names `tracker-drift` under its pinned slug and names `BL-001`,
  the work item left standing above the generated mirror marker.
- It is precise, not merely loud: `BL-002`, the mirrored item *below* the
  marker, is not flagged — that region belongs to the companion that writes
  it, and the check leaves it alone.
- `zero_writes` held in audit mode with the new check present: `git status`
  and `HEAD` byte-identical before and after.
- Restructure routed the finding to `## For the team:` and left
  `docs/backlog.md` byte-unchanged, which is the law: migrating a work item
  is a write outside the repository, and this skill never makes one.
- `parity_every_check_has_a_defect` passed with 18 checks: the new slug has
  its planted defect, and no defect exists for a check the law does not pin.
