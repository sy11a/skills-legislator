   `not-law`-suppressed statement in its closing notes. v20's record captured

3. **Six full corpus attempts were made; five did not finish.** Two were killed
   by tmpfs quota exhaustion (BL-059) and three found law defects. Only the
   sixth is reported here. Attempt-level history is in the chronicle below.
4. **The law changed five times during the cycle.** Each fix resets the law
   generation, so no intermediate run is comparable to this one and none is
   quoted as a result.
